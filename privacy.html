Kazoekata privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Kazoekata is an iPhone app for studying Japanese counters. It is published by Joni Quintos. This page explains what the app stores and what it does not.

Study data stays on your iPhone

The app keeps your study progress on the device only. That includes counters you mark learned, favorites, quiz scores, readings you missed, the daily goal, the streak, the sound setting, and light or dark mode.

There is no account. This study data is not sent to the developer. The app does not show advertising and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Deleting Kazoekata deletes the study data. Installing it again starts from the beginning.

The one-time purchase

Full Access is a one-time purchase. Apple processes the payment. Apple’s privacy policy covers that transaction: apple.com/legal/privacy.

The app uses RevenueCat to confirm the purchase and to restore Full Access on the same Apple ID. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user id and the App Store receipt. It uses the receipt to confirm the purchase and for its own purchase records. It does not receive your study progress, your name, or your email address, and it does not track you across other apps. RevenueCat’s privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Restore Purchases brings back Full Access only. It does not restore study progress.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No advertising
  • No tracking across other companies’ apps or websites
  • No sale of personal information
  • No access to contacts, photos, location, or the microphone

Children

Kazoekata is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. The version linked from the App Store listing is the current one.
