Kazoekata support

Kazoekata is an iPhone app for studying Japanese counters. The first 15 counters and a short quiz are free. Full Access is a one-time purchase that opens all 40 counters and the full quiz. It is not a subscription.

Purchases

Apple processes the payment. Restore Purchases is on the Unlock sheet, on the home screen and on any locked counter. Restoring brings back Full Access on the same Apple ID. It does not restore study progress, which stays on the iPhone.
